Grouped by bill title, in bill order. One row per account (former names resolve into it); a rollup heads the accounts it totals, which fold away under it. Totals are shown only where a printed total is on file. Each cell's headline is the account's budget authority for that year and stage; + more opens every other line on file for the cell (supplemental, rescission, transfer, component lines) — shown, never summed in. The six cell states (Not funded / None, No printed total, Missing, Not yet collected, Not yet enacted, or a figure) mean exactly what they mean for one account; the count of each is shown above the grid. ▸ source shows where a figure was printed. A dashed outline marks a year or stage outside the account's records — before its first figure or confirmed absence.
